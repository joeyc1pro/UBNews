Iran Vows to Keep Strait of Hormuz Closed

March 2026 — UBNews Middle East Desk

Iran issued its first statement under its new supreme leader, Mojtaba Khamenei, vowing to keep the Strait of Hormuz closed and continue attacks on U.S. bases in the region. The statement was broadcast internationally with remarks read on Iran state media.

The announcement comes as attacks on commercial ships have spread in regional waters, oil prices have risen above $100 a barrel, and military tensions continue to escalate. Pentagon reports note casualties among U.S. forces and civilians in the region.

The United Nations refugee agency has reported that as many as 3.2 million Iranians have been displaced due to ongoing conflict, while hundreds have been killed across neighboring countries. The new leadership's stance emphasizes continued resistance to foreign military presence.

Analysts suggest that Iran’s declaration reinforces the region's instability, with global shipping and energy markets closely monitoring further developments. Experts warn of potential disruption to maritime traffic through this vital chokepoint for global oil exports.
