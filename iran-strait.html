Iran Vows to Keep Strait of Hormuz Closed

March 12, 2026 — UBNews Middle East Desk

Iran has issued a statement declaring its intention to maintain restrictions on shipping through the Strait of Hormuz as tensions in the region continue to rise.

Officials warned that foreign vessels must follow new authorization procedures before entering the strategic waterway, which handles a significant portion of the world’s oil shipments.

International observers say any prolonged disruption in the strait could have serious consequences for global energy markets and maritime trade.

Naval forces from several nations remain deployed nearby as governments monitor the situation and assess the impact on international shipping routes.
