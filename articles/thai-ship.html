Thai Cargo Ship Attacked Near Strait of Hormuz

March 11, 2026 — UBNews Maritime Desk

A Thai-flagged cargo ship was struck during rising tensions near the Strait of Hormuz, according to maritime security officials monitoring the region.

Rescue teams responded after distress signals were received from the vessel. Most crew members were safely evacuated by nearby naval forces.

The ship reportedly suffered damage while traveling through a high-traffic shipping corridor used by commercial vessels transporting goods and energy resources.

Authorities have launched an investigation to determine the cause of the attack and whether naval mines or other weapons were involved.

Shipping companies operating in the area are closely monitoring security advisories as tensions remain elevated.
