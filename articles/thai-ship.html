Thai Cargo Ship Attacked Near Strait of Hormuz

March 12, 2026 — UBNews Maritime Desk

A Thai-flagged bulk carrier was struck by missiles near the Strait of Hormuz as regional tensions continue to escalate.

The vessel, identified as the Mayuree Naree, was traveling from the United Arab Emirates toward India when projectiles struck the ship above the waterline, triggering explosions and a fire in the engine room.

Twenty crew members abandoned ship in liferafts and were rescued by naval forces in Oman, while search efforts continued for several crew members who were believed to have remained onboard.

Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps later claimed responsibility for the attack, stating that vessels must seek permission before transiting the strait.

The Strait of Hormuz remains one of the most important shipping routes in the world, carrying a large share of global energy exports each day.
