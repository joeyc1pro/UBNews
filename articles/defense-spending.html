US Defense Spending Sees Largest Increase in Decade

March 10, 2026 — UBNews Washington Desk

Congress has approved a defense spending package totaling roughly $875 billion, marking the largest increase in military funding in more than ten years. Lawmakers say the budget is intended to modernize equipment, strengthen cyber defense capabilities, and maintain military readiness.

Officials said a significant portion of the funding will go toward advanced technologies, including next-generation fighter aircraft, missile defense systems, and improved space surveillance capabilities.

The Department of Defense also plans to expand research into artificial intelligence and autonomous systems, which analysts say will play an increasingly important role in future military operations.

Supporters of the measure argue that the funding increase reflects growing global security challenges and the need to maintain technological advantages.

Critics, however, say the size of the budget highlights ongoing debates about federal spending priorities.
