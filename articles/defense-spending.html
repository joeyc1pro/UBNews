US Defense Spending Sees Largest Increase in Decade

March 11, 2026 — UBNews Defense Desk

The United States Congress has approved an $875 billion defense budget, marking the largest increase in military spending in more than a decade.

The funding package focuses heavily on modernization programs, including next-generation aircraft, missile defense systems, cyber capabilities, and naval expansion.

Lawmakers supporting the measure argue that rising global tensions and rapid technological advancements by rival nations require a stronger and more modern military.

The budget also includes funding for troop readiness, training operations, and improvements to military infrastructure across the country.

Critics of the spending plan argue that such large increases may place additional strain on the federal budget and call for greater oversight of defense contracts.
