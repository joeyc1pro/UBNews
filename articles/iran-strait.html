Iran Vows to Keep Strait of Hormuz Closed

March 12, 2026 — UBNews World Desk

Iran has issued a statement attributed to its new supreme leader declaring that the Strait of Hormuz will remain closed to certain international shipping.

The announcement was broadcast on Iranian state television alongside a photo of Mojtaba Khamenei, who Iranian media described as the country’s new leader.

The message warned that attacks against U.S. military bases in the region would continue as the conflict escalates.

International organizations report that thousands of people have been displaced across the region as fighting spreads and tensions rise.

The Strait of Hormuz is one of the world's most important energy corridors, and any prolonged closure could significantly disrupt global oil markets.
