Iran Vows to Keep Strait of Hormuz Closed

March 12, 2026 — UBNews Middle East Desk

Iranian officials issued statements warning that restrictions could be placed on vessels entering the Strait of Hormuz as tensions in the region continue to rise.

The strait serves as one of the world's most important maritime chokepoints for global energy trade, with millions of barrels of oil passing through the waterway every day.

Diplomatic analysts say any attempt to restrict shipping through the route would likely have significant economic consequences.

Several governments have called for restraint and urged all sides to avoid actions that could disrupt international shipping.

Security conditions in the region continue to be closely monitored by naval forces and international observers.
