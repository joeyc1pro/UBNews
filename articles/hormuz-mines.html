Mine-Laying Operations Reported in Strait of Hormuz

March 12, 2026 — UBNews Maritime Desk

Reports from maritime security agencies indicate that naval mines may have been deployed in the Strait of Hormuz, raising concerns about the safety of commercial shipping in the region.

The narrow waterway is one of the most strategically important maritime routes in the world, carrying roughly a fifth of global oil exports each day.

International naval forces have increased surveillance operations in the area while merchant vessels are being advised to exercise extreme caution when transiting the strait.

Defense analysts say mine threats can significantly disrupt global shipping lanes and require specialized naval units to detect and remove them safely.

Officials have not confirmed which actor may be responsible, but tensions in the region have been escalating amid the broader conflict involving Iran and Western allies.
