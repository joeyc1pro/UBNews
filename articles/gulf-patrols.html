International Naval Coalition Expands Patrols in Persian Gulf

March 13, 2026 — UBNews Defense Desk

Naval forces from several allied nations have increased patrol operations throughout the Persian Gulf as tensions surrounding the Strait of Hormuz continue to escalate.

Military officials say the expanded patrols are intended to deter potential attacks on commercial shipping and ensure safe passage for oil tankers traveling through one of the world's most important maritime chokepoints.

The coalition presence includes guided-missile destroyers, surveillance aircraft, and specialized mine-countermeasure vessels capable of detecting and neutralizing naval mines.

Defense analysts say multinational patrols help distribute security responsibilities across the region while allowing participating nations to share intelligence and maritime surveillance data.

Several countries with large energy imports have expressed concern about potential disruptions to tanker traffic and have supported increased naval coordination.

Officials say the patrol operations will remain in place as long as security conditions require additional monitoring of commercial shipping lanes.
