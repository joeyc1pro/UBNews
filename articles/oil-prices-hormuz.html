Global Oil Prices Surge Amid Hormuz Shipping Fears

March 13, 2026 — UBNews Energy Desk

Global oil prices rose sharply Friday after growing security concerns in the Strait of Hormuz prompted shipping companies and energy traders to reassess the safety of tanker routes through the strategic waterway.

Energy analysts say the market reaction reflects fears that any disruption in the narrow corridor could significantly impact global energy supplies. Roughly 20 percent of the world's oil passes through the strait, making it one of the most important shipping routes in the international energy market.

Insurance costs for tankers operating in the region have also increased as maritime risk assessments are updated following reports of naval mines and attacks on commercial vessels.

Several shipping companies have reportedly delayed voyages while awaiting updated security guidance from naval forces currently operating in the Persian Gulf.

Market strategists say even limited disruptions could cause noticeable increases in fuel prices worldwide if tanker traffic slows or insurance premiums continue rising.

Officials monitoring the region say the situation remains fluid, and naval patrols have increased to help ensure commercial shipping lanes remain open.
