US Defense Spending Sees Largest Increase in Decade

March 12, 2026 — UBNews Military Desk

Congress has approved an $875 billion defense budget, the largest in a decade, to modernize military equipment and enhance cyber defenses.

The funds support both domestic and international commitments, including joint operations with allied nations, and focus on new technologies to counter emerging threats.

Officials say the increase reflects concerns over global instability and the need to maintain strategic military readiness.
