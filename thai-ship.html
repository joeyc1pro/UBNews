Thai Cargo Ship Attacked Near Strait of Hormuz

March 2026 — UBNews Maritime Desk

A Thai‑flagged bulk carrier, the Mayuree Naree, was struck by Iranian missiles near the Strait of Hormuz on Wednesday. According to the Royal Thai Navy, 20 Thai crew members were rescued by the Royal Navy of Oman while three remained on board.

The vessel, operated by Precious Shipping Plc, departed Khalifa Port in the UAE en route to Kandla in India. The attack occurred after the ship transited the strait at about 11:10am Thailand time, hitting the engine room and igniting a fire.

All 23 crew members were Thai nationals. The 20 who abandoned ship in liferafts were brought safely ashore at Khasab in Oman. Omani naval units worked to assist the three crew members still on board.

The incident comes amid heightened instability in the region, with Iran claiming responsibility for two attacks, including the Mayuree Naree. Iran’s IRGC stated that vessels must get permission before passing through the strait.

Maritime security firms report that at least 14 vessels have been struck since late February. The Thai Navy is coordinating through international channels to ensure the safe return of all crew members. Precious Shipping said the vessel was insured and no cargo was lost.
