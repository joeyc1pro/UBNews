Thai Cargo Ship Attacked Near Strait of Hormuz

March 12, 2026 — UBNews Maritime Desk

A Thai-flagged bulk carrier was struck by missiles near the Strait of Hormuz during escalating tensions in the region. The vessel, identified as the Mayuree Naree, was traveling from the United Arab Emirates to India when the attack occurred.

According to reports from the Royal Thai Navy, most of the crew abandoned ship in lifeboats and were rescued by naval forces operating nearby. Several sailors remained on board as emergency teams worked to control fires and stabilize the vessel.

The attack comes as security concerns in the Strait of Hormuz continue to rise, with several vessels reportedly targeted or damaged in recent weeks.

International maritime authorities are monitoring the situation closely as shipping companies reassess safety conditions in the region.
